## Not very cybersecurity of them!

We have been preparing a real treat for you.
The keen eyed may have noticed some channels on our Discord going *missing*...
This has been a consolidation effort to merge as much as possible into one channel.
This channel has been bridged to the [OFTC IRC network](https://www.oftc.net/).
This means that you can participate on either platform and not be left out!

The club will likely **never abandon Discord**.
It is the common denominator platform and the Cybersec Club *exists* for the layman.

# IRC Presence

Join the bridge on the OFTC IRC network on `#cucybersec`.
Before you can participate you must register with NickServ.
You may use this [webclient](https://webchat.oftc.net/?channels=cucybersec) or any other IRC client.
Let's not ruin a good thing :)

# 2026 CyberSci Applications
Hello everyone! It's that time of year again!
[CyberSci](https://cybersci.ca/) regionals is happening in exactly one month, November 21st.
We are now opening up the applications for Carleton's two teams.
You may apply [using the following form](https://forms.gle/Zkndg7RMhVXZxS2U9).

Hope to see your submissions!

Make sure to [join the CyberSci Discord](https://discord.gg/XXyUuHjZKp) if you have any questions.
